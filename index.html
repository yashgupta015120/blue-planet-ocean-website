Protect the blue.
Restore the balance.

Marine conservation works best when science, communities, and everyday choices work together. These are practical ways to help a healthier ocean become more than a hope.

⌁

Protect ocean habitats

Support well-managed marine protected areas and restoration projects for coral reefs, mangroves, salt marshes, and seagrass meadows.

HABITATS

Refuse unnecessary single-use items, reuse what you can, sort waste correctly, and join safe, organised shoreline or river clean-ups.

Choose ocean-friendly food

Ask where seafood comes from, follow trustworthy local sustainability guidance, and avoid products linked to destructive or illegal fishing.

SHELTER · NURSERY · DIVERSITY● ECOSYSTEM FIELD NOTE

Coral reefs pulse.

Built by tiny coral animals over generations, reefs provide food and shelter for a remarkable variety of sea life. They can also reduce wave energy along some coastlines.

✳

Why it matters

Reefs create complex homes for fish and invertebrates and support coastal livelihoods and local food webs.

⌁

How to protect it

Never stand on or touch coral, use reef-safe wildlife practices, reduce emissions, and support science-led reef protection.

“A living city beneath the waves.”
✳

Marine environments are connected by currents, food webs, and climate. Conservation plans should respect local communities and be guided by regional science.
